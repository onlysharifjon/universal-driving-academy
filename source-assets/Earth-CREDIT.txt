Earth texture: NASA Visible Earth / NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble (2002).
Composite satellite imagery showing land surface, ocean color and sea ice.
Image: Reto Stockli / NASA Goddard Space Flight Center.
Original texture (2048 x 1024):
https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/57000/57730/land_ocean_ice_2048.jpg
Downloaded mirror from the official three.js example assets:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mrdoob/three.js/dev/examples/textures/planets/earth_atmos_2048.jpg
Source and background:
https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/the-blue-marble-true-color-global-imagery-at-1km-resolution/

This is an illustrative rotating Earth, not a live satellite feed or real-time weather.
Rendered in WebGL with spherical UV mapping, directional lighting and atmospheric rim.
earth-fallback.webp is an orthographic spherical projection of the same texture,
used if JavaScript or WebGL is unavailable. It is generated by source-assets/build-earth-fallback.py.
